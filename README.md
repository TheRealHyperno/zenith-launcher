## Zenith Launcher
### When Nova meets Oasis.

Zenith Launcher is a performant, good looking launcher for your Android device. It is what happens when a minimalist meets the users.

You still have your custom good lock icons (Tested - OneUI 8.5, SM-S921W / S24 - Canadian Model) and everything, but its packed in your panel. Zenith launcher features caching, so your launcher can provide a quick and speedy experience without disrupting your workflow.

Features:
- Theming
- Application specific widgets
- Ad-free experience
- Custom Zenith widgets
- App styling
- And more!
